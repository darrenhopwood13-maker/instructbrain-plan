Defensible evidence

Why our reports refuse to guess, and why that is the point on a job where the document ends up on a table between two parties.

What usually happens

Software that reads photographs will give you an answer for every photograph. Ask it about a shot that shows nothing useful and it still returns a confident line, because a blank answer reads as the tool having failed.

So the report looks complete, and one of its lines is a guess. On a schedule of condition or a due-diligence report, that is the line the other side finds.

What we do instead

It will not guess

Where the evidence does not support a judgement, the item is marked not assessed and says so on the page. It does not resolve to a pass.

It may only cite a standard that is on its list - 12 Approved Documents, 6 British and EN standards, 6 HSE instruments. Nothing off that list is ever cited, and if nothing on the list fits, it cites nothing at all.

A person has to resolve it

The report cannot be issued while anything is unresolved. A named person confirms, corrects or deletes each item, and each item needs a trade before it can go.

Publishing freezes the document: the issued version keeps its reference and its date, and a change starts a new version rather than editing the old one.

Why it matters here

A snagging list is a to-do list - speed is the point, and a machine filling in every line is welcome. A schedule of condition or a dilapidations report is not a list, it is a position: it gets used at lease exit, in negotiation, in provision reporting, sometimes in front of a lawyer.

One invented line and the document stops being evidence. An item that openly says not assessed, and here is why - resolved by a person, with the photograph, the time, the place and the capture details attached - is the thing that survives being challenged.

What holds it up

The ruleWhat it means in the report
Not assessedAnything the evidence cannot support is flagged for a person, never resolved to a pass
Citation ceilingOnly the listed standards may be cited; no invented clauses and no guessed paragraph numbers
Evidence attachedEvery photograph carries its own time, place and capture details, and every finding sits on the photograph it came from
Confirmed by a personThe report will not issue with an unassessed item in it, and every item carries a trade
Frozen on issueThe issued version never changes afterwards; a correction becomes a new version with its own reference

What it does not do

Read this before relying on the output

  • It does not decide engineering. It describes what the photograph shows; severity and sign-off are a person's call.
  • It is not a drawing tool - no CAD, no BIM, and it does not replace a measured survey.
  • It cannot assess what was not photographed, and it will say so rather than fill the gap.
  • It does not send anything by itself. Distribution is a person's decision.
  • It is not a substitute for professional judgement, and it is not a certification of compliance.

If a report has to survive being challenged, the honest blank is worth more than the confident guess.
