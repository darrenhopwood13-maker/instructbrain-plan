Everything on the hub

Every page in one list, with what each one is for. Eight of these were finished and live but not linked from the front page, which is how a finished thing goes missing the day you need it.

1For today's session

The running order for the room: what to photograph, what to say, and what to have open.

  • IB-DEMO-SHOTS-001 — Demo photographs — what to shoot The three reports for the demonstration, and the exact photographs each one needs. Tick them off as you go.
  • IB-DEMO-001 Rev 4 — The demo, start to finish The run sheet: the beats in order, the screen to open, the words to say, and how to recover if the room goes quiet.
  • IB-PRES-001 Rev 2 — The presentation The five-minute story with real screens in every slide. Read the speech on a phone while the slides are up.
  • IB-CAP-001 Rev 1 — Capability dossier Everything the product does in one place, including what it deliberately will not do.
  • samples — Report samples Sample reports as they come out of the product.
  • IB-REV-001 — Trade & subcontractor allocation: an independent review What is built, what is not, and the risks, each marked confirmed or partial with the evidence.

2Clips and codes

Short clips, one per task, filmed on the live product. Send the link; nothing to sign into.

  • 13 clips — Help clips — watch the page Every task from setting the directory up to correcting an issued report. Under thirty-five seconds each, narrated, pointer on screen.
  • QR — QR code for the slide · bare code Scans to the clips page. Highest error correction, so it survives a projector.
  • voice — Help clip voice samples The two voices the narration was chosen from.
The help clips, on a phone Point a camera at this, or open darrenhopwood13-maker.github.io/instructbrain-plan/help/. Thirteen clips, one task each — nothing in them created a report, changed a trade or sent anything.

3The evidence

For when somebody asks how you know, rather than what it does.

  • IB-LEAF-001 — Defensible evidence — why the report refuses to guess The case for an assessment that abstains, and what happens when it does.
  • specimen — Report layout, now built in The report's own layout and the rules behind it.
  • IB-PROMO-001 — The flashes Two pages: every feature and guardrail as a one-line flash. No pricing, no certification claimed.
  • IB-WALK-001 — The directory, to the firm's extract A screen recording of the live product: one firm's work, rolled into one extract.
  • page — Real reports, from real photographs The pitch page.

4Everything else

  • IB-MAN-001 — The reporting area: operator manual How a report is made, reviewed, published and sent - enough to hand the app to somebody cold.
  • films — Two films — instructBrain and instructSite Built from real product screens, captions burned in.
  • IB-WALK-002 — Post-publish verification walk What was claimed, how each claim was proven, and what the walk caught.
  • IB-PLAN-002 Rev 3 — Three changes, planned, built and live Trade allocation as a switch, one press to confirm, and the readability work a real report asked for.
  • IB-PLAN-001 Rev 3 — The action plan — the front page The standing plan and what is left with you.
