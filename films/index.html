What is real, and what is not

  • Real product screens. Every instructSite frame is the live instructsite.ai, captured at double resolution. The instructBrain frames are the product's own pages and its real issued report pages, rendered from the source PDFs at 300 DPI.
  • Real report pages. The report imagery is from the sample reports, whose projects and addresses are fictional by design. No client project appears in either film.
  • Synthetic. The narration is machine-generated (edge-tts, British male). The music bed is machine-generated too. The two short pieces of site footage are AI-generated - the figures are wide or back-to-camera, never a face.
  • Every claim is the product's own. The citation ceiling (12 Approved Documents, 6 British and EN standards, 6 HSE instruments), "not assessed where the evidence does not support a judgement", and instructSite's own lines are quoted from the products themselves.
  • Playing a video inside a chat app re-encodes it and it will look softer there. These files are the masters - watch them here or download them.
