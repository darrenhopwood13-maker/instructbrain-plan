Before they walk in — two minutes

  1. Sign in at instructbrain.com as the Demonstration account. Check the header says Demonstration account — if it says anything else you are in the wrong organisation and the demo data is not there.
  2. Open three tabs, in this order: Reports (the list), SW-2026-10-001 (Site condition 11 – 13 Bruton Street), and the register from Projects.
  3. Open SW-2026-10-001 on its second tab — “2 Get PDF”. That is where Publish and Share live. Nine demos out of ten go wrong here: they are not on the first tab.
  4. Have one printed report page in your hand. A paper report in the air beats any slide.

Draft — the photograph becomes the writing

1:45–2:10
ShowSW-2026-10-001 → tab 1 Photos & findings → open one item. Point at the drafted finding text, the severity and the suggested trade.
SayIt drafts what’s there, what it means, what to do, and the likely cause when the picture supports one. It sets a severity and suggests a trade. “And everything it writes is marked as a draft, because that’s what it is.”
CarefulDo not call it “the AI decides”. Say “it drafts; a person decides”. They will quote you back.
6

The gate — nothing issues unconfirmed

2:10–2:45
ShowThe confirm controls on the findings list — and if you have a draft open, the line the app itself shows: “Not ready to publish yet: N still not assessed, N with no responsible trade, N not confirmed by a person.”
SayEvery item is confirmed, corrected or deleted by somebody with a name. It will not publish while anything is unassessed — a hard gate, not a warning banner. Where the evidence doesn’t support a judgement it says not assessed and a person has to resolve it.
CarefulThis is the slide to slow down for. Two beats longer than feels natural. Never drop this one, whatever the clock says.
If asked“What if the AI is wrong?” — It can’t publish anything by itself. A wrong line is caught by the person confirming it, and that is the point of the gate.
7

The citation rule

2:45–3:20
ShowAn item in SW-2026-10-001 that carries a reference — point at the regulation line.
SayIt may only cite what is on the list. Twelve Approved Documents, six British and EN standards, six HSE and statutory instruments. It picks the single most relevant, from that list and nowhere else — and “if nothing on the list fits, it cites nothing at all.”
CarefulPause before “no invented clauses” and let the room be quiet. This is the sentence the whole product rests on.
8

The output

3:20–3:45
ShowThe printed page in your hand. On screen: SW-2026-10-001, tab 2 Get PDF → Open the report PDF. Cover first: project, client, address, reference, date, status, item count.
SayThen item by item — numbered, graded, the action required, the photograph it was written from, and a pin marking the area. Whatever the job runs to.
If asked“How many pages?” — Say what you’re holding. Real reports run 20 to 40-plus pages, and more pages is the price of never splitting an item across a page break.
9

Frozen — what was issued stays issued

3:45–4:05
ShowThe report’s published version, and the line on its cover: “Published as version 1 on <date>”. Point at that, and at the version list beside it.
SayPublishing locks it. Change something later and it starts a new version — the issued one stays exactly as it went out, with its number and its date. “If somebody asks in three years what that report said, that’s what it said.”
If asked“So which version does my client have?” — The one you sent them. A share link hands back the version it was made against, so a reissue never moves the copy in their inbox. Making them a new version means sending a fresh link, deliberately.
10

Compliance — built, not typed

4:05–4:25
ShowProjects → the project → its compliance register. In the demo account the weekly registers are there (fire, housekeeping, scaffold, welfare, excavation, lifting).
SayWeek by week, the checks that were done, built from the same evidence as the reports — not reconstructed into a spreadsheet the week before an audit. “It’s the screen an auditor asks for, and it’s already there when they ask.”
CarefulThe register is a list of weeks and checks — not a chart that draws itself. Do not promise a graph.

Breadth

4:25–4:45
ShowSlide 11 — or, better, the template chooser on screen: Start a report → the chooser, five families, twelve templates.
SayTwelve templates in five families — condition, snagging and handover, site and safety, M&E, and records and registers. Thirty languages, including Arabic and Hebrew right to left, with English always kept as the record copy. Plus the trade and recipient directory, distribution, per-trade links, your own branding on the document.
CarefulYour slide says “eleven report types”. The app now shows twelve templates in five families — the twelfth is the certificate register. If somebody is counting, say twelve templates, or say “eleven report types plus the certificate register”. Don’t get caught on your own number.
CarefulIt is a list. Keep it moving — don’t let the breadth slide become the speech.
12

The limits, and the close

4:45–5:00
ShowSlide 12. Then stop.
SayNot a drawing tool — no CAD, no BIM, no estimating. It doesn’t decide engineering: severity, trade and sign-off are a person’s. It won’t invent a citation. And it sends nothing by itself — distribution is an action somebody takes.
CarefulLand “thank you” and stop. Do not add a sentence after it. The limits are why they trust the rest.

If you get cut to three minutes

Drop cards 9 and 11. Cut card 5 to one line: “the photograph becomes the writing, and everything it writes is a draft.” Never drop card 6 or card 7 — the gate and the citation rule are the two things nobody else in the room can say.

Questions you will get

They askYou say
Is it just ChatGPT writing my reports?No. It drafts, and a person confirms every item before anything can be published. The gate is hard: unassessed or unconfirmed, it will not issue.
How do I know it isn’t making the regulation up?It can only cite from a fixed list — twelve Approved Documents, six standards, six HSE and statutory. Outside that list it cites nothing at all. That is the rule the product is built on.
What if the AI gets something wrong?It cannot publish by itself. The person confirming is the control, and the item is confirmed by name.
Can somebody edit it after it’s gone out?No. What was issued stays issued, byte for byte. A change is a new version, and you decide whether the client gets it.
How many report types?Twelve templates in five families — condition, snagging, site and safety, M&E, records and registers. Thirty languages.
Does it work offline / on site?It’s built for a phone in one hand, in the rain. Photographs keep uploading as you go, and catch up when you have signal.
What about the client’s own branding?Your organisation branding goes on the document. The report is yours.
Will it send to subcontractors for me?It prepares each trade’s own extract with their own items only, and it does not send anything by itself — somebody presses send.
How much does it cost?Do not invent a figure. Say you will send the commercial detail, and move on.
Does it do drawings, CAD, take-off, estimating?No, and it isn’t trying to. It replaces the paperwork after the walk, not the drawing office.

If it goes wrong, in front of people

They are all recoverable. Stay calm, and say the honest line.

The Share menu “isn’t there”. You are on the first tab. It lives on 2 Get PDF. Say “let me get to the document view” and carry on.

“Create link” is greyed out. That report is not published yet — the app will not make a client link to a draft. Publish it first, or switch to SW-2026-10-001 which is published. Say: “it won’t link a draft, by design.”

A link opens a web page instead of the PDF. That is the one fix still waiting on your publish. Say: “the link hands over the finished document” and show the PDF from Open the report PDF instead. Rehearse the link demo after you publish.

Publish is refused. Something is unassessed or unconfirmed. Read the line out loud — it names what is missing — and turn it into a feature: “this is the gate.”

Something is slow or refuses to load. Say “give it a moment, it’s a live system”, then talk to slide 3 while it catches up. Never tap a control twice in front of an audience.

Three things not to do

Don’t publish live in the demo unless you mean to — it is a real record with your name on it, and a published version cannot be unpublished.

Don’t promise that sending works end to end. Emailing extracts has not yet been used on a live job. If someone asks, say it is built and being proved on the first job — that is the honest answer and it costs you nothing.

Don’t show a draft and call it finished. The register drafts and DEMO-PC-001 (reopened, so not currently published) are the two places a sharp question could land.

Three lines that must land

1. “Nothing issues that a person has not confirmed.”

2. “It may only cite what is on the list — and where nothing fits, it cites nothing at all.”

3. “What was issued stays issued.”

If you remember nothing else off this sheet, those three are the meeting.
