Demo photographs - what to shoot

Three reports for the 15 October demonstration. Tick them off as you go.

Report 1Health, safety & housekeeping8 photographs

Opens the demonstration. Health and safety is what a technical director is personally accountable for, so it is the first thing they should see working.

  • A fire extinguisher, in date, on its bracket
  • A blocked fire escape route or a fire door propped open
  • Missing edge protection - open edge, no guard rail
  • A tidy stair core or a tidy walkway
  • Temporary lighting, in place and working
  • Trailing leads or a damaged lead
  • The welfare setup - canteen, washing, drinking water
  • The first aid point or the accident book

Plus one deliberately unclear photograph - too far away, oblique, or half out of frame. This is the beat that lands hardest: the assessment looks at it, declines to judge, and asks for a person instead of guessing.

Report 2Snagging, distributed by firm10 to 12 photographs

The headline. One main subcontractor per trade, one extract per firm. Spread the snags so at least three firms get work, and keep one snag that belongs to nobody in particular.

Premier Interiors - four
  • A badly taped joint or a crack at a plasterboard joint
  • A missed paint coat, bare patch or paint on the face of a fixture
  • Tile lippage, or a tilde line that runs out
  • Skirting out of level, or a mitre that does not close
Apex Envelope - three
  • A failed mastic or sealant joint around a window or door
  • A brick or block defect - spalled, cracked, or a perp out of line
  • A roof outlet, upstand or rainwater detail
MEP Solutions - two or three
  • An unsealed penetration through a fire-rated wall or ceiling
  • A socket or switch out of level, or a damaged faceplate
  • A valve, pump or AHU that plainly wants commissioning
ABC Civil Engineering - one or two
  • A level, fall or compaction problem in the slab or the external works
  • A damp-proof course breached or bridged
Nobody allocated - one
  • Anything genuinely hard to place: general site cleanliness, temporary protection, damage with no obvious trade

Report 3Photo condition record, plant & external5 photographs

Short. It proves the product works where there is no inside to walk.

  • The plant room, as a whole
  • A roof outlet or a roof upstand
  • External drainage, a gully or a channel
  • Blockwork or render to an external wall
  • A louvre, vent or external grille

How to shoot them

  • Landscape, every time. The reports lay photographs out in landscape.
  • One subject per photograph. Two defects in one frame become one finding, and a half-right finding is worse than none.
  • Close enough that the defect is the subject. If you can see the whole elevation, the camera was too far back.
  • Nothing staged. Do not tidy up before a photograph, and do not create a defect to photograph. The demonstration is that it reads the site as it is.
  • Do not worry about getting them right. A photograph the assessment is unsure about is useful - it is half the story.
Send them over in batches named H&S, snagging and plant. Each batch becomes its own report, analysed and ready to show. Nothing is sent to anybody - every item stays a draft until a person confirms it.
