4Three things that need your decision, not my guess

6 The two old reports that both say “Reference: 001” YOU decision
The facts29 reports on the account. Two still carry the reference 001. Both were issued before references were built in the database, so they hold the hand-typed value.
Why it mattersTwo different issued reports are indistinguishable by reference. But a report that has already gone to a client is a record - rewriting it is not a small thing.
Your options(a) Leave them and show a small “recorded by hand” note on screen - the wording already exists in the code; (b) reissue them with new references; (c) do nothing.
My recommendation(a). It is honest, it changes no issued document, and it stops the next person being confused.
7 Delivery webhooks are not configured YOU needs a URL
What's wrongSettings says “Not configured - bounces will not be recorded”. So when a report email bounces, you never find out.
What I need from youWhere the bounce notifications should land - a provider (Resend, SendGrid, Svix) or an endpoint of your choosing.
What I'll doBuild the wiring and the test once I know the destination. I cannot invent the endpoint.
Done whenA test bounce is recorded and visible.
8 Sign-in emails still come from Supabase's shared sender YOU needs SMTP
What's wrongPassword and sign-in emails go out from Supabase's shared sender rather than your own domain. That reads as less trustworthy to a client, and shared senders get throttled.
What I need from youWhich route - your own SMTP (for example Resend or SendGrid) plus the DNS records for instructbrain.com.
What I'll doWire it, send a test, and show you the headers.
CostSmall monthly cost depending on volume. I will quote it before anything is signed up to.
