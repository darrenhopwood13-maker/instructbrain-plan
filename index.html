7Delivery webhooks are not configured NEEDS YOU needs a URL
What's wrongSettings says “Not configured — bounces will not be recorded”. When a report email bounces, nobody finds out.
What I needWhere the notifications should land — a provider (Resend, SendGrid, Svix) or an endpoint of your choosing. I cannot invent the destination.
8Sign-in emails still go out on a shared sender NEEDS YOU needs a decision
What's wrongReport emails are properly authenticated through Resend (SPF, feedback MX and DKIM all present on instructbrain.com). Sign-in emails are not: there is no notify. sending domain, so they run on the default sender with its hourly cap.
Two routes(a) Lovable’s own email feature — paid plans only, DNS handled for you. (b) Point Supabase Auth’s SMTP at Resend — your auth already lives in your own Supabase project, the free tier covers it, and you already have the Resend account. I recommend (b): it costs nothing.
