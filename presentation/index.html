IB-PRES-001 · Rev 11 / 12

The problem

The paperwork happens twice.

  • Photographs get taken on site — and then re-filed from the camera roll at nine at night.
  • Findings get written up from memory, hours after the walk, in a Word template.
  • References get typed by hand. Page numbers get typed by hand.
  • A regulation gets quoted from memory — and it may be the wrong one.
  • Then it goes by email, and the version in somebody's inbox is the version.

Step one

It starts where you are standing.

Shot 4
The capture screen on a phone — the field cockpit with a photograph just taken.

Open the cockpit from a QR code. It installs to the home screen and opens full-screen, because it is going to be used in one hand, in the rain, wearing gloves.

Each photograph is filed against the element, room or location it belongs to as it is taken. Those location values stamp onto the photograph and become the location the report prints.

Nothing is renamed, re-sorted or emailed to yourself afterwards.

IB-PRES-001 · Rev 14 / 12

Step two

The photograph becomes the writing.

For each photograph, the product drafts a finding: what is there, what it means, what to do about it, and the probable cause where the image actually supports one.

It sets the severity and suggests the trade responsible. It does not decide either.

Everything it writes is a draft, and it is labelled as one.

Shot 5
A finding opened with its drafted text, severity and trade — before anyone has confirmed it.
IB-PRES-001 · Rev 15 / 12

The gate

Nothing issues that a person has not confirmed.

Shot 6
The findings list with the confirm controls visible — unconfirmed items still outstanding.

This is the difference between a tool and a liability. The machine writes; a person decides.

Every item is confirmed, corrected or deleted by somebody with a name. The report will not publish while anything is still unassessed or unconfirmed — it is a hard gate, not a warning banner.

And where the evidence does not support a judgement, the item is marked not assessed and has to be resolved by a person.

IB-PRES-001 · Rev 18 / 12

The record

What was issued stays issued.

Publishing locks the report. Reopening it does not quietly change what went out — it starts a new version.

The issued version is kept exactly as it was issued, with its number and its date. If somebody asks in three years what the report said, you can produce it, byte for byte.

Report references are allocated by the database, so two reports cannot claim the same one.

IB-PRES-001 · Rev 19 / 12

Compliance

The register is built, not typed.

Shot 10
The weekly compliance register, with its weeks and its checks.

A project carries a weekly compliance register alongside its reports: the checks that were carried out, week by week, against a fixed window of weeks.

It is built from the same evidence as the reports, as the checks are recorded — not reconstructed into a spreadsheet the week before an audit.

That is the screen an auditor asks for, and it is already there when they ask.

IB-PRES-001 · Rev 111 / 12

What it does not do

The limits, stated out loud.

  • It is not a drawing or modelling tool. No CAD, no BIM, no estimating.
  • The machine drafts language; it does not decide engineering. Severity, trade and sign-off are a person's.
  • It will not invent a citation — so some findings cite nothing, deliberately.
  • It sends nothing by itself. Distribution is an action somebody takes.
