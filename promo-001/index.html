The product

  • Photograph in, draft finding out. The AI writes the finding, the severity and a suggested trade from the photograph itself.
  • It shows its working. Every suggestion carries a confidence figure and a reason in plain words. No reason, no suggestion.
  • It is allowed to say "I don't know". Where a picture doesn't support an answer it abstains, and Not assessed stays a real outcome rather than becoming a pass.
  • A person confirms. Nothing else does. One press settles the obvious ones - only unassigned items with a suggestion at 80% or better. Everything else waits for a human. new
  • It may only cite from a closed list. Never a clause, paragraph, section or table number - the document only. Where nothing on the list clearly applies, it cites nothing at all.
  • 13 report templates, 5 families. Snagging & handover, Condition & fabric, Site & safety, M&E, Records & registers.
  • A health, safety & housekeeping inspection. A site-safety record in its own right: housekeeping, welfare, PPE, fire and escape, routes, paperwork. Deliberately not a building-fabric survey, and it won't offer a legal opinion. new
  • 30 languages, per report. A survey can exist in several languages without being redrawn.
  • A tone for every room. Formal, easy-going, sharp, meticulous, sarcastic - or short and sweet, one plain line per item. new
  • Published means frozen. A published report's link serves the issued PDF. Reopening a report to correct it never disturbs the link a client already has.
  • Every door out obeys one rule. Share link, PDF, print, subcontractor extract - the same frozen copy, every time. new

Getting it to the right firm

  • Per-trade extracts. Each subcontractor receives only their own items, with a footer that says exactly that: "This extract lists only the items attributed to [trade] on [report]. It is not the full report."
  • One firm, several trades. A groundworks firm covering drainage, civils, concrete and below-ground utilities is one company against four trades - and gets one extract, not four. new
  • Internal labour is a first-class route. Your own labour and principal-contractor items have a home of their own, instead of being disguised as a missing answer. new
  • Set up once, reuse everywhere. Build the project's trade and subcontractor map, save it as a template, and the next job starts with it already in place.
  • A pre-flight before anything leaves. Unassigned items, missing mappings, missing contacts and confidential findings are all shown before a single send. new
  • Nothing sends itself. Every send is one deliberate press and is recorded against the report - who sent what, to whom and when.
  • Confidential is enforced in the database. A finding involving a person cannot reach a subcontractor. Not hidden on a screen - blocked by the database itself.

How it runs, and what it will not do

The second page: the flow, the three promises, and the answers to the awkward questions.

Six steps, start to finish

  • Capture. Photograph on site, from a phone, into the job.
  • Analyse. The AI drafts each finding, its severity, its trade suggestion - with confidence and reasoning shown.
  • Confirm. A person accepts, edits or rejects. Trades and grades are confirmed separately, so nothing is quietly settled on your behalf. new
  • Publish. The report is issued as a frozen version. That version is what every link serves, for ever.
  • Distribute. Extracts go to the right firms, deliberately, one press at a time - or as a trade link with no account needed.
  • Record. Who confirmed what, who sent what, to whom and when. An audit trail you can read.

Three promises you can make safely

1Nothing issues that a person hasn't confirmed.
2It may only cite what is on the list - and where nothing fits, it cites nothing at all.
3What was issued stays issued. The link a client has keeps serving the same document.

What it will not do

It won't rate anyoneNo subcontractor scoring, no league tables, no performance rankings. It records what was observed and who was appointed - nothing more.
It won't decide liabilityA trade suggestion is a suggestion. Responsibility is confirmed by a person, every time.
It won't act on its ownNo automatic sends, no silent first-match guesses. Where the answer is ambiguous, the ambiguity is shown and a person resolves it.

If they ask

  • "Isn't this just ChatGPT?" No. The AI may only choose from the trades and citations the job already defines, it must show its confidence, it must abstain when unsure, and nothing it writes is final until a person confirms it.
  • "How do you know it isn't inventing the regulation?" It cannot cite a clause number at all, and it may only select a document from a closed list. If nothing applies, it returns nothing.
  • "Who can see our data?" Organisation boundaries and role permissions are enforced in the database, not just the interface. One organisation cannot see another's records.

Where it is strong today, and where it is honest

  • Strong: capture to confirmed report, the citation rule, the frozen published copy, the audit trail, the per-trade extract, the weekly compliance registers.
  • Being proved: live emailing of extracts end to end. The plan, the extracts and the send log are built and demonstrable; the first live job proves the delivery.
  • Coming: one extract per firm rolled up across all of its trades, and package boundaries where one trade is split across firms by zone or floor.
